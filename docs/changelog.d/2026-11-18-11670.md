- Invoices, the invoice editor: an exit now asks "Close without saving?"
  before it throws away an edited draft or a typed Storno reason (system_3
  #11670, §3.166). Esc, the scrim, the ✕, Close and Back all ask, and so does
  a review's Next on an invoice with a typed reason; discarding there moves
  on to the next invoice. Each draft of a review, and each one just accepted,
  rejected or kept for later, moves on without asking. While a step is being
  saved, no exit closes the editor, the ✕ included.
