- **Invoices: a client can carry notes** (system_3 #9987, the client half of
  #9962; operator c61516). The Add/Edit client dialog has a Notes field,
  saved with the client and never printed on an invoice or included in an
  export. In the Clients list a note shows as one muted line under the name,
  cut with an ellipsis; the full text is in the editor. More than 5000
  characters gets the server's refusal, with the text kept in the field to
  shorten.
