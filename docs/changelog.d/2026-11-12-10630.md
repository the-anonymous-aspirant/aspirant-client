- Invoices: Transactions lists money in and out together, newest first,
  in one row format (system_3 #10630, design §3.143 Decision 3). Each row
  reads who, what it was for, the category, for money in the client and
  its invoice ("Invoice 2026-014", "Draft" or nothing), and the signed
  amount in the Book's ink; the source ("N26", "PayPal", "By hand") sits
  on the muted date line. A filter, All · In · Out, leads a category
  select whose options follow it. A hand-entered payment's method line no
  longer repeats "Entered by hand".
