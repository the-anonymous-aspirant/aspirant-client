- Invoices: the expense and payment forms ask before an exit throws away
  what was typed (system_3 #11668, A1 of #11632; design conventions §3.166).
  Esc, a press outside the dialog, the corner ✕, Back and Cancel close at once
  when nothing is unsaved. When something is, the footer asks "Close without
  saving? What is not saved yet will be lost." with "Keep editing" and
  "Discard changes", and the form waits as typed. A picked receipt counts.
  While a save runs, no exit closes the form. Back now closes the open form
  and stays on the page, and a reload with unsaved input raises the browser's
  own prompt. The shared piece (`unsentGuard.js`, `UnsentAsk.vue`) is what
  the app's other dialogs take next.
