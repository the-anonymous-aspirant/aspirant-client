- Invoices: a payment or expense imported from an N26 statement or CSV export
  is no longer named by an id the server made up ("csv-…", "stmt-…"). It is
  named by its date and "who · what": on its checkbox, "Generate invoice",
  "Open draft", the Book's card button, the removed view, and the invoice's
  "Covers" and "Payments covered" lines. A provider's own id (PayPal, or an
  N26 transfer confirmation) still names its row. The checkbox and
  "Generate invoice for …" of a hand-entered row now use its date and
  "who · what" too, as its other controls already did, instead of its
  internal id (system_3 #12257).
