- Invoices: the invoice list folds by month, drafts first (system_3 #10818,
  design §3.145). "Drafts · 2" comes first, then one fold per issue month,
  newest first ("November 2026 · 4 invoices"); a cancelled invoice stays in
  the month it was issued. Drafts and the newest month start open and older
  months start folded; a list with a single group shows it open. While a
  search, a status or a date filter is set, every group is open, and
  clearing it brings back the folds from before. A month ze opens stays
  open when the list reloads, and an invoice just saved or issued opens its
  month and scrolls into view. "Select all N issued shown" is now one
  labelled control above the list on desktop as on phones; it counts the
  open groups only, and folding a group unticks its invoices. On desktop,
  every month's table has the same column widths, so the columns line up.
