- Transactions: you can see which payments have no invoice yet (system_3
  #11020, UX walk AM3; design rulings c73490, c73497, c73504, c73520 and
  c73534 on #11020).
  Before, a payment with no invoice was marked only by a missing line. Every
  payment now says its invoice state in words. With a live invoice it shows
  the invoice or "Draft", as before. With none, Paid work and Uncategorised
  read "Not invoiced", in body ink, and Gift, Loan, Refund and Other read "No
  invoice needed", muted. The PayPal transfer into N26 keeps its reason line.
  On a phone card, "Not invoiced" sits beside "Generate invoice", or ends the
  category line ("Uncategorised · no client · Not invoiced"), so no card
  grows. A new "Invoice" filter after the category select, hidden under Out,
  lists each state with its count over every month. Select all says what it
  leaves out ("Select all 4 of 5 shown · 1 invoiced"), and when nothing can be
  selected it says so ("All 2 shown are invoiced, so none can be selected.").
  Nothing new is fetched. To keep two cards on a 390×844 first screen with
  the new filter, the gaps above the list are 4px on a phone, on every tab
  (they were 12px). Where a tab opens on plain text (Invoices, the Book),
  that text sits 8px under the tabs.
