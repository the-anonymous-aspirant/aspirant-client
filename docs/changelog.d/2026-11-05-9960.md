- **Invoices: tick invoices in the list and download them as one PDF**
  (system_3 #9960, the client half of #9956; operator c61402). Issued rows
  in the Invoices list carry a checkbox. A third Export button, "Invoice list
  (single PDF)", downloads the ticked invoices as one `Rechnungen.pdf` in
  invoice-number order, after the same check as the other exports. A caption
  under the button says how many are ticked. Drafts and cancelled invoices
  have no checkbox, and one line above the list says why. A refused download
  now gives the server's reason, for example which invoice was cancelled,
  instead of a generic line, and that holds for the CSV and ZIP exports too.
