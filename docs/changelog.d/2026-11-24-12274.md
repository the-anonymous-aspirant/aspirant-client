- Invoices: a draft says what issuing needs, and stops on a difference
  before it is issued (system_3 #12274).
  - **Marks from the moment it opens:** an empty Description reads "Needed
    to issue: say what the work was.", and a quantity or a price that is
    zero or empty has its own line. A missing client, service date or line
    is marked the same way, as are a period that ends before it starts and
    a day after today.
  - **The press sends nothing while a mark shows.** Accept, "Issue invoice…"
    and "Issue now" move focus to the first marked field instead.
  - **The total and what the draft covers:**
    - "Total €300.00" under the lines, with the net and the VAT when VAT
      applies;
    - "Covers 1 payment: €300.00 from Dragan Petrovic, 2026-10-03", with
      one line per payment and a sum when there are several;
    - each line wraps, so a phone no longer scrolls sideways.
  - **A difference is said at once.** "The total (€35.00) differs from the
    payments it covers (€350.00)." shows as ze types. Once ze leaves the
    field, Accept reads "Accept with this difference" and takes a second
    press; a double tap is not one.
  - **Refusals in the app's words:** every refusal of the issue reads its
    own sentence, from the server's figures. For example, "This invoice
    totals €300.00. Above €250.00 an invoice needs the client's postal
    address. Add it under Clients." The server's sentence is no longer
    shown.
  - **In a review**, the address note says "Press Later to keep this draft,
    then add the address under Clients."
  - **A draft that covers no payment** has no "Issue invoice…".
