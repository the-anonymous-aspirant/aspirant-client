- Admin: the Christmas cards, an admin-only picture puzzle with a new tile on
  /admin. Play shows one card at a time. The right word under it, typed
  with any capitals and spaces, opens the next card, and after the last
  card the closing sentence is shown with the cards under it. A wrong word
  keeps the card, and a check that fails says so rather than calling the
  word wrong. Control lists the cards with their words hidden until "Show
  answers" is ticked. It shows each card's state (solved and when,
  current, or locked) and lets you replace a picture, edit a word or the
  sentence, or go back to an earlier card.
