- Invoices: the Book and Transactions fold by month, newest first
  (system_3 #10636, design §3.145). Both lists group by the month the
  money moved, the newest month on top and the newest day first inside it.
  The newest month starts open and older months start folded; a list with
  a single month shows it open. On the Book the month line with its In,
  Out and Result is the fold itself, so a folded month still shows its
  result. On Transactions each month reads "November 2026 · 12". A filter
  opens every month and clearing it brings back the folds from before.
  After a save, an import or a restore, the months the rows landed in open
  and the newest row scrolls into view, and an import says where its rows
  went ("The new rows are in September–November 2026."). "Select all N
  shown" is one labelled control above the months on desktop as on phones;
  it counts open months only, and folding a month unticks its rows. On
  desktop every month's table has the same column widths.
