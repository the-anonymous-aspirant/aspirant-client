- Invoices: the issuer profile editor gained a Logo field (system_3 #9860,
  from #9858; server #9859). A 140×72 preview tile on white paper, the
  printed logo box's proportions, shows the current logo, with Upload or
  Replace and Remove. The change is staged like every other field and sent by
  Save before the profile, so Cancel discards it. A logo the server refuses
  saves nothing and its message shows under the field; a saved logo with a
  refused profile is reported as a partial save. The logo prints top right on
  every invoice issued after the save, and issued invoices keep theirs.
