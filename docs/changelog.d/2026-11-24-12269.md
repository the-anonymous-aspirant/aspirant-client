- **Invoicing: the charts' current month is the month in Berlin** (system_3
  #12269). The invoices chart and the Book's chart took "this month", the one
  their captions call "so far", from the UTC clock. For the first hour or two
  of a month they still ended on the month before. Both now use the Berlin
  month, through `berlinMonthKey` beside `formatBerlinDay`.
