- Invoices: the Book counts only the categories she chose (system_3 #10635,
  design §3.143 Decisions 7 and 8). The starting selection, "Work only",
  counts payments marked Paid work and every expense except Private, so
  payments marked Gift, Loan, Refund, Other or Uncategorised leave the Book
  and its CSV. A muted line under the head row says what is counted. When
  Uncategorised payments are left out, it says how many, and "Review them"
  opens Transactions on In plus Uncategorised. "Change what counts" holds a
  preset select and Money in and Money out ticks; Private is never offered.
  A changed tick applies at once and reads "(changed)". She can save the
  ticks as her own preset, update it, or delete it (the delete arms first).
  "Work only" cannot be changed or deleted. The CSV button is captioned with
  the selection ("Uses Work only"). Transactions flags each row the Book
  does not count with a muted "Not in the Book".

  (design_agent c67318 on system_3 #10635). The caret comes from one shared
  `.fold-summary` class, and the browser's own marker is hidden, so each
