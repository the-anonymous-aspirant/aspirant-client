- Invoices: a month view, and a month's invoices generated and reviewed one at
  a time (system_3 #11505, epic #11497 items 1 and 2d; design §3.159). "Work
  done in" leads the toolbar and filters the list to the invoices whose work
  was done that month; the tab opens on the newest month that still has
  paid-work payments without an invoice, or a draft waiting. "Generate
  invoices for {month}" is the tab's one primary and says before the press how
  many payments it will draft and how many drafts already wait. The server
  drafts one invoice per payment (aspirant-server #11504) and the editor walks
  them as "Invoice 2 / 7 · client": Accept saves what was typed and issues it
  in one press, under a sentence that says so; Reject deletes the draft; Later
  keeps it. Pressing again picks up the drafts left over. A line above the
  toolbar then says what the review did. Outside a review, issuing keeps its
  two presses.
