- **Invoices: delete any payment that is not on an issued invoice**
  (system_3 #10010, from #9973, operator c61587/c61868; server #10009). The
  Payments tab's two-step Delete is now offered on imported payments as
  well as hand-entered ones, and on payments on a draft or cancelled
  invoice. Armed, the row says what the delete does: an imported payment
  will not come back when the same file is imported again, and one on a
  draft or cancelled invoice comes off it (a draft keeps its lines). A
  payment on an issued invoice says "On an issued invoice — cancel that
  invoice first", and has no selection checkbox (nothing can act on it);
  select-all counts only the rows it can act on.
