- Book: a PDF of the entries, beside the CSV (system_3 #11512, #11497 item
  13; the operator's c70883 on #10985). The Export group has a second button,
  "Book entries (PDF)", under the CSV's, and the "Paid in" select scopes both
  files. The file lists each month the Book shows, newest first, with its In,
  Out and Result and then its entries: date paid, who and what for, category,
  in and out, with a minus on every Out figure. Under its title line it says
  what the Book counts and how many payments are still Uncategorised. A month
  with no entries says "No entries", and a file of several months closes with
  their totals. The caption under the button says how many entries the file
  will hold. The file is made in the browser from what the tab shows, is
  named like the CSV (`book-entries-<selection>[-month-YYYY-MM]-<day>-<time>.pdf`),
  and is available once the Book has loaded.
