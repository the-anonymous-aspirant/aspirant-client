- **Invoices: statistics for clients, income and invoices over time**
  (system_3 #10005, from #9972, operator c61586). The Invoices tab's chart
  became a Statistics block of three charts stacked on one month axis, with
  each month at the same position in all three. They show clients over time
  (a running total by the date a client was added, as a stepped line),
  income per month in euros (issued invoices by issue date, cancelled ones
  left out), and invoices issued per month, as before. The current month is
  labelled "so far". With no clients and no issued invoices, one line says
  so.
